摘要

CatRun 1.0.1 是给 iPhone 的单人、离线优先跑酷。设计制作为 Zhichao Wei，App Store 销售方为 Shanghai Qishan Cultural Communication Co., Ltd.。商品名是 CatRun: Neon Market Rush / CatRun：晨市冲刺，Apple ID 6793595842，Bundle identifier 为 com.weizhichao.catrun。现已上架的版本不包含广告 SDK、第三方分析 SDK、账号系统或跨应用追踪。App Store 隐私问卷的答案是：本应用不收集数据，不追踪，不使用广告标识符。

  • 不外传游戏进度、设置和本地事件不会发送给开发者，也不会卖给任何人。
  • 无广告没有广告网络。救援来自主页每天一次的免费机会。
  • 无账号不登录 Game Center，不建立玩家档案，不把分数发到公共榜。

Summary

CatRun 1.0.1 is a single-player, offline-first iPhone runner designed by Zhichao Wei and sold on the App Store by Shanghai Qishan Cultural Communication Co., Ltd. The listing names are CatRun: Neon Market Rush and CatRun：晨市冲刺. Apple ID 6793595842. Bundle identifier com.weizhichao.catrun. The live build has no advertising SDK, no third-party analytics SDK, no account system, and no cross-app tracking. The App Store privacy answers are: this app does not collect data, does not track, and does not use the advertising identifier.

  • Nothing is sent out Progress, settings, and local events stay on the iPhone. They are not sold.
  • No ads There is no ad network. Rescue is the once-a-day chance on Home.
  • No account Game Center sign-in stays off. Scores are not posted to a public board.

保存在你设备上的信息

为了记住猫咪、装扮、街道修复、任务、季节标记、鱼干与铃铛、音乐音效触感开关，以及一条未结束的路线，CatRun 会在这台 iPhone 上写入一份本地进度文件。文件使用设备钥匙串中的密钥做完整性签名，并保留一份备份副本。这些数据留在设备上，开发者收不到副本。

应用还会在本地保存最近最多 100 条游戏事件，例如应用启动、完成教程、完成路线、使用免费救援。每条事件只有短名称和有限的游玩数字（分数、鱼干、闪避、猫咪编号）。这些记录写在 UserDefaults 里，不会上传，也不能用来做跨应用追踪。

加密的 iPhone 备份可能会带回上述文件。CatRun 不是云存档。把备份恢复到另一台 iPhone 时，签名校验可能失败；此时会保留现有文件并提示，而不会用空白进度覆盖。

Information stored on your device

CatRun writes a local progress file so it can remember cats, outfits, street tiles, missions, season marks, Fish and Bells, music and haptic preferences, and a route that is still live. The file is signed with a key in the device Keychain and kept with a backup copy. The developer never receives that file.

The app also keeps at most the last 100 gameplay events on device, such as app start, tutorial finished, run complete, and a free rescue. Each event has a short name and a few play numbers (score, fish, dodges, cat id). The list lives in UserDefaults. It is not uploaded and cannot track you across apps.

An encrypted iPhone backup may restore that file. CatRun is not a cloud save. Restoring onto another iPhone can fail verification. In that case the existing file is kept and shown with a notice, instead of being replaced by an empty market.

我们不收集的内容

  • 姓名、邮箱、电话、精确位置或通讯录。
  • 支付卡号。CatRun 是免费游戏，也收不到卡信息。
  • 广告标识符、指纹或跨应用/跨网站追踪信号。
  • 发往开发者服务器的崩溃日志、分析包或聊天内容。CatRun 1.0 没有自己的服务器。

What we do not collect

  • Name, email, phone number, precise location, or contacts.
  • Payment card numbers. CatRun is free and never receives card details.
  • Advertising identifiers, fingerprints, or cross-app or cross-site tracking signals.
  • Crash logs, analytics packs, or chat sent to a developer server. CatRun 1.0 has no server of its own.

Apple 服务

你从 App Store 下载 CatRun 时，Apple 会按自己的隐私条款处理下载、收据和设备备份。如果你通过系统对话框与 Apple 分享诊断，崩溃信息由 Apple 处理；CatRun 不会另外接到一份开发者分析后台。

CatRun 1.0 是免费游戏，没有真实货币购买或订阅，也不会登录 Game Center。若以后版本加入购买或其他联网能力，会先更新本政策和 App Store 隐私披露。

Apple services

When you download CatRun from the App Store, Apple processes the download, receipt, and device backups under Apple’s own terms. If you share diagnostics through a system prompt, Apple handles that report. CatRun does not run a separate developer analytics console.

CatRun 1.0 is free. It has no real-money purchases or subscriptions and does not sign you in to Game Center. If a later version adds purchases or other online features, this policy and the App Store privacy disclosure will be updated first.

本机接口

隐私清单声明了两类本机接口，都只在设备上使用：

  • UserDefaults（原因代码 CA92.1），用来保存进度、设置和本地事件。
  • 系统启动时间（原因代码 35F9.1），用来给跑酷循环和音效排队计时。这个值不会发送出去，也不用来识别你。

应用声明不使用需要出口合规说明的非豁免加密。钥匙串里的签名密钥只为本地存档防篡改服务。

On-device APIs

The privacy manifest lists two on-device APIs, both used locally:

  • UserDefaults (reason CA92.1) for progress, settings, and the local event list.
  • System boot time (reason 35F9.1) to time the run loop and sound queue. The value is not sent away and is not used to identify you.

The app declares that it does not use non-exempt encryption that would need an export filing. The Keychain signature key exists only to detect a tampered local save.

儿童

CatRun 不会故意收集儿童或任何其他人的个人信息，因为它根本不收集个人数据。产品按 4+ 休闲游戏来设计，不是儿童类别应用。监护人可以使用 iPhone 的屏幕使用时间来限制使用。

Children

CatRun does not knowingly collect personal information from children or anyone else, because it does not collect personal data at all. The game is built as a 4+ casual title, not a Kids Category app. A parent can limit use with Screen Time on the iPhone.

你的选择

可在设置中重置这台 iPhone 上的本地进度，或删除应用。如果设备开启了 Apple 备份，重装后信息可能被恢复。该备份不是有保证的云存档。音乐、音效和触感可以随时关掉，不影响存档。

Your choices

Reset local progress from Settings, or delete the app. If the device owner has an Apple backup turned on, reinstalling may restore the file. That backup is not a guaranteed cloud save. Music, sound, and haptics can be turned off at any time without wiping the market.

若未来版本加入联网功能、广告、购买、账号或改变这些做法，会在发布前更新本政策与 App Store 隐私披露。生效日期仍从 2026 年 7 月 22 日算起；最近一次按已上架的 1.0.1 源码与 App Store 隐私答案复核是 2026 年 9 月 19 日。
